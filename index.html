AI Settings

On-device AI (default): a small open-source model (Apache-2.0 licensed, free for commercial use) runs entirely on your phone via WebGPU — no internet, no account, no cost, once the one-time model download finishes. Quality is modest compared to large cloud AI, and it needs a device/browser that supports WebGPU.

Offline Smart Search (always available): a free keyword-matching fallback with no AI and no download — used automatically if on-device AI isn't supported on your device.

Optional cloud key: for higher-quality answers you can paste your own Anthropic API key below. This needs internet and uses your own paid credits — it is not free and not required.

Note: none of these are a substitute for a qualified lawyer, accountant, or doctor — treat answers about legal, financial, or medical documents as a starting point, not professional advice.
